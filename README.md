# 🐦 PIGEONDOM: NYC
### The Rise of the Pigeon King

**A New York City survival adventure about perseverance, growth, and bouncing back.**

> "You don't become king because you never fall. You become king because you never stop getting back up."

---

## 🎮 About the Game

**Pidgeondom: NYC** is a top-down survival adventure set in New York City. Players take control of Pip, a young pigeon born with only one leg, who is rejected by his flock because he is different.

Alone in the streets of New York, Pip must collect breadcrumbs, survive dangerous encounters, grow stronger, and overcome challenges to achieve his ultimate dream: becoming the King of all pigeons.

His journey takes him from the sidewalks of NYC to the rooftops of the Empire State Building, also known as **Pigeon Tower**.

The game is inspired by the hackathon theme:

**BOUNCING BACK.**

## 📖 The Story

Pip was born different.

While his siblings could walk, run, and fly with ease, Pip struggled to keep up. Eventually, he was left behind and forced to survive on his own.

As a juvenile pigeon, Pip discovers breadcrumbs scattered throughout the city. Each breadcrumb gives him experience and allows him to grow bigger and stronger.

However, New York is not an easy place to survive.

Humans, squirrels, rats, and raccoons threaten Pip's survival. Encounters with these animals can frighten him, causing him to lose breadcrumbs and some of his growth.

But every setback is another opportunity to bounce back.

As Pip grows, he discovers new territories, makes allies, faces rival pigeon flocks, and eventually challenges the reigning ruler of Pigeon Tower.

His ultimate goal is to become the Pigeon King.

- **Squirrels:** Territorial competitors that chase Pip away from food.
- **Rats:** Subway inhabitants who know the underground city.
- **Raccoons:** Unexpected enemies that emerge from trash cans.
- **Pigeons:** Other flocks that Pip must eventually earn the respect of.

### Main Characters

- **Pip:** The one-legged protagonist determined to become king.
- **Remy:** A streetwise rat who helps Pip navigate the subway.
- **The Crown:** The reigning pigeon king and Pip's ultimate rival.

## 🍞 Core Gameplay Mechanics

### Breadcrumb Collection
Breadcrumbs are the primary collectible and progression resource.

- Collect breadcrumbs to earn XP.
- Accumulate XP to level up.
- Grow physically as Pip gains experience.
- Use breadcrumbs as currency for certain items.

### Growth System
Pip begins as a tiny juvenile pigeon.

As he gains XP, he becomes larger and stronger, gradually progressing through different stages of life.

### Bounceback System
Getting scared or defeated causes Pip to lose some accumulated growth and carried breadcrumbs.

However, permanent progression allows Pip to become stronger even after setbacks.

The goal is to make every restart meaningful rather than making players feel like their entire journey was wasted.

### Survival
Players must monitor their basic needs:

- Health
- Hunger
- Thirst

Players can find or purchase food and water to survive.

## 💡 The Message Behind Pidgeondom

Pip's journey is about more than becoming the biggest or strongest pigeon.

He begins as an outcast because of something he cannot control. Rather than allowing that difference to define his future, he learns to use it as his strength.

His one-legged hopping eventually becomes his signature ability.

**You don't have to be like everyone else to accomplish something great. You just have to keep moving forward.**
